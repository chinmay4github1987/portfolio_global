About

I build the app — and increasingly the server behind it.

I started writing Android in 2018 and never really stopped. Most of what I have shipped since falls into two worlds — ecommerce, where a dropped payment is a real customer lost, and edutech, where tens of thousands of students open the app on the same morning and expect it to just work.

Day to day that means Kotlin and Java against a Jetpack stack: MVVM, Coroutines and Flow, Room for offline, Retrofit at the edges. Lately I share business logic through a Kotlin Multiplatform core and own the backend it talks to — Spring Boot microservices behind an API gateway, persisted in PostgreSQL, with live tracking pushed over WebSockets to the customer, vendor and delivery apps at once.

Currently at Test Yantra Software Solutions in Bangalore, where I coordinate a seven-person cross-functional squad. I work from design handoff through release and keep an eye on the crash dashboard afterwards. Comfortable working remote, and available for freelance builds alongside it.
